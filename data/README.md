# data/

| Directory | Contents |
|---|---|
| `cases/` | not tracked; `make cases` downloads the case corpus from [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jxtse/RI-Bench) |
| `interventions/court.jsonl` | one record per Court case: the planted procedural-fact error, the anchor and replacement text in each side's excerpts, and the mechanical checks it passed |
| `interventions/parliament.jsonl` | one record per Parliament inquiry: the two opposed witnesses, each claim's original and qualified wording, and the added limitations |
| `generated_reviews/<design>.jsonl` | one record per OpenReview case and arm: the reviewer assignment (stance, score, argument style) and the generated initial and post-rebuttal reviews |
| `human_annotation/` | human calibration of the semantic scorer |

Interventions and reviews were generated by GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Sonnet 5 respectively and checked before any subject
ran (paper, Appendix B). Paper texts and author rebuttals are not duplicated here; the bundle builders take them from
`cases/`.

## Generated reviews

| File | Design | Arms |
|---|---|---|
| `open_review.jsonl` | real-vote | A: majority well-argued, B: minority well-argued |
| `open_review_v3.jsonl` | constructed-vote (also used by `or_r`, `or_e2a`, `or_e3c`) | C: control, X: adversarial |
| `or_e2b.jsonl` | 2:2 count-balanced control | SP, SM |
| `or_e3a.jsonl`, `or_e3b.jsonl` | 2:1 and 4:1 majority controls | X |

## Human annotation

Three annotators (A1–A3), none of them authors, independently labeled 100 Court rulings, 100 Parliament reports (200
witness slots) and 30 generated reviews. Annotator identities are replaced by A1–A3; the files are otherwise their
exports. `machine_labels.json` holds the semantic and pattern labels of the same items with the episode they came from.
`interfaces/` contains the offline HTML annotation tools as given to annotators. `tools/human_calibration_stats.py`
computes the agreement statistics reported in the paper's appendix.
